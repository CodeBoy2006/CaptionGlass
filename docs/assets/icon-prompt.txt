Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final Android adaptive launcher foreground and repository brand icon for CaptionGlass, an Android on-device bilingual floating-caption app.
Primary request: Design one exceptionally refined, instantly recognizable premium icon: a single floating rounded rectangular slab of smoked emerald optical glass, with two inset horizontal caption strokes, the upper stroke longer and pearl-white, the lower stroke shorter and pale mint. Style: bespoke industrial product render, restrained and architectural, almost frontal with a very subtle depth bevel, optically polished edges, satin translucent body, soft internal emerald light, elegant controlled highlights. Existing brand is forest green #1B6B50 and dark obsidian #0E1311. No purple, no neon.
Composition: square 1024 x 1024 transparent canvas. Exactly one mark centered; the whole mark INCLUDING the handle, bevel and soft shadow fits inside the central 600 x 560 pixels, leaving generous fully transparent margin for Android adaptive icon cropping. Slab horizontally wider than tall. Two caption strokes remain bold and distinct at 48px display size.
Background: actual alpha transparency, no baked background tile, no rounded-square app container, no scene, no floor.
Constraints: no letters, no words, no watermark, no microphone, no speech bubble tail, no play triangle, no circuit motif, no glitter, no extra objects, no collage. Create the production icon itself, not a presentation mockup.
